Our Story

About ElectraInnovation

A passionate team from Satara dedicated to making technology education accessible, practical, and impactful for every student and professional.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between theoretical education and industry-ready skills by providing hands-on, project-based technology training that empowers students to build, innovate, and lead.

Our Vision

To become Maharashtra's most trusted technology education centre — creating a generation of skilled engineers who drive innovation at every level of industry and society.

Our Values

Hands-on learning over passive instruction. Practical outcomes over certificates. Mentorship over lectures. We believe every student can become an innovator with the right guidance.

How It Started

From a Small Lab to a Growing Community

ElectraInnovation was founded in Satara, Maharashtra with a simple belief: the best way to learn electronics is to build something real. Our founders noticed that most engineering graduates had strong theoretical knowledge but struggled to translate it into working projects.

Starting with a small batch of 12 students, we ran our first Arduino workshop with nothing but enthusiasm, a handful of microcontroller boards, and a determination to make learning tangible. That first batch produced three working robots and a home automation prototype — and we were hooked.

Today, we have trained over 250 students, conducted 30+ seminars at colleges and institutions across Maharashtra, and supplied educational kits to dozens of schools and training centres. But every session still feels like that first workshop.
