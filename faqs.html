Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courses, services, kits, and how to get started. Can't find your answer? Reach out anytime.

Courses

No prior experience is required for our beginner courses like Basic Electronics and Arduino Programming. We start from the fundamentals. For intermediate and advanced courses, a basic understanding of electronics or programming is helpful — we'll let you know the prerequisites on each course page.

Most of our courses are conducted in-person at our Satara centre, as hands-on lab work is a core part of the learning experience. Some courses like Python for Engineers are available in a hybrid format (online theory + in-person lab sessions). Contact us to discuss what works for you.

Yes. All students who complete a course and build the required project receive a Certificate of Completion from ElectraInnovation (Electronexus). This certificate is recognised by partner colleges in Maharashtra and demonstrates practical, project-based skills to potential employers.

We keep batches small — typically 8 to 15 students — to ensure every participant gets adequate attention, lab time, and personal mentorship. This is one of the key ways we differ from large coaching institutes.

Refunds are available up to 3 days before the course begins. Once components and kits have been prepared for your batch, we cannot offer a full refund, but we can transfer your enrollment to the next available batch at no extra cost.

Kits & Equipment

Yes! For all hands-on courses, the educational kit with all required components is included in the course fee. You also get to keep the kit after the course ends, so you can continue building projects at home.

Absolutely. We supply educational kits directly to students, colleges, and training centres. Each kit includes all components, a component guide, and project ideas. Contact us for pricing and bulk orders.

We offer Arduino starter kits, ESP32 IoT kits, robotics kits, sensor assortment kits, PCB design starter packs, and custom kits built around specific project goals. Bulk discounts are available for institutions ordering 10 or more kits.

Seminars & Services

Yes — we regularly conduct seminars at colleges and schools across Maharashtra. We handle the entire programme, including demos, materials, and interactive activities. Contact us with your preferred dates and topic areas and we'll send you a proposal within 48 hours.

Popular seminar topics include: Introduction to IoT, Embedded Systems Trends, Robotics and Automation, AI Hardware and Edge Computing, PCB Design Fundamentals, Career Paths in Electronics Engineering, and more. We can also design a custom seminar around your institution's curriculum needs.

Yes. We offer end-to-end mentoring for final year B.E./B.Tech projects — from selecting a topic and writing the proposal, to building the prototype and preparing documentation. We have guided dozens of students to successful project completions.
